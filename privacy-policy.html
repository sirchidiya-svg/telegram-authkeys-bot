Privacy Policy & Transparency Notice

This document explains how AuthKeys Generator Bot (@AuthKeys_Bot) handles, stores, and protects user data, authentication keys, and transaction logs. We strictly uphold a zero-knowledge approach for your credentials through client-isolated cryptographic encryption.

1. Core Principles

🔐
Military-Grade Encryption

All saved keys and descriptions are encrypted at rest with AES-128-CBC via Fernet cryptography before touching persistent disk.

🚫
Zero Tracking or Ads

We do not profile your usage, sell data to advertisers, or scrape messages. We only process commands you trigger.

🗑️
Total Data Sovereignty

You retain full right of erasure. Execute /delete_all_my_data at any time to purge all your records immediately.

2. Information We Collect and Process

When you interact with the bot through direct messages, group chats, or inline queries, we only process the technical data necessary to perform the requested operation:

Data Field Source & Purpose Storage State
user_id & chat_id Telegram numeric identifiers used to enforce daily quotas, subscriptions, and team permissions. Plaintext (Primary Keys)
Saved Key & Details Your custom labels, API credentials, and saved generated keys. Fernet Encrypted (Ciphertext)
Payment Charge ID Telegram Stars charge identifier used to verify subscription validity and process refunds. Indexed for audit & refunds
Inline Queries Ephemeral generation requests triggered by @AuthKeys_Bot. Never stored / Discarded instantly
Feedback Submissions Feedback messages, bug reports, and screenshots submitted via /feedback. Routed to Admin & not retained in DB

3. Telegram Stars & Refund Policy

All monetary transactions are carried out using Telegram Stars (XTR). AuthKeys Bot does not process, receive, or store your credit card numbers, bank details, or personal billing addresses.

Self-Service Refund System:

Subscribers may request a refund at any point directly within Telegram using the /paysupport command. Upon confirmation, the bot alerts our administration team with your verified charge identifier. Once authorized, Telegram's API instantly returns your Stars to your balance and your subscription is terminated.

4. Your Rights & Data Erasure

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and Telegram Developer Standards, you hold unconditional rights over your stored content:

  • Right to Access / Portability: Export all records created within the current chat using /export_my_data.
  • Right to Rectification & Deletion: Delete individual titles with /delete {title}.
  • Right to Complete Erasure: Purge every single record attached to your account across all chats permanently using /delete_all_my_data.

5. Group Chats & Team Sharing

When added to Telegram groups, team sharing is disabled by default. Only verified group administrators can toggle sharing on or off using /team_sharing on|off. When enabled, team members can retrieve group-scoped saved entries, with the contributor's display name clearly credited.
